## Open choices

Before their implementation/activation, resolve or qualify:

- Compatible exact Node/pnpm/TypeScript/Electron/React/Expo/native/compiler/PG versions, OS floors/architectures, redistribution licenses and clean-device resource limits. No new pins are silently inherited from another codebase.
- Engine G1 comparison, OpenShell-first G2 conditions or equally qualified alternative, each real permitted Codex/Claude route/plan and G4 all-auxiliary-call/no-overage evidence. A candidate is not an adopted working integration.
- Account/Email service implementation, operating budget/abuse controls and deployment; maintained auth/crypto/native key/clock/lock/peer libraries and tests.
- Pairing/relay/push maintained protocol and service, metadata/privacy/cost and actual-device behavior. Framework choice does not select hosted Expo services.
- Document/import/export engine/font/license tuple and fidelity bounds; complete import/source synchronization. OCR/PPTX remain separately qualified.
- Exact full-release invitations/admin continuity/staffing, communication/history and plugin package/data/bridge schemas beyond the minimum packet.
- Performance objectives for persistent assistants versus queued work/concurrent Runs, CPU/GPU/model/browser resources and user attention. No1000-concurrent-Run claim.
- Exact source license, contribution/assets/dependency compatibility, trademark clearance, production signing/release/hosting policies and actual revenue offers.

Reversible implementation details within settled requirements should be decided by the delivery agent with evidence. Ask the founder only for consequential scope/security/cost/authority choices, one concrete question at a time. Do not reinterview settled platform/product choices without new requirements or failure evidence.
