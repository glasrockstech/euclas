# Product and scope

Euclas helps people build and operate organizations through persistent digital people. A company owner, parent, individual or club organizer begins with one assistant, gives it real work, and develops roles/teams only when useful. The organizational unit is the continuing person/responsibility/work relationship, not a transient chat session or a particular model.

## Complete initial scope

The complete initial product includes general-public no-Terminal use and developer depth within the same authority model:

- Persistent assistants with names/pins, governed private/role/project/organization memory, directly addressable at any authorized level; routine quality review/escalation by authorized supervisors.
- Companies, households, clubs and personal work; human colleagues invited with independent official Accounts, scoped Memberships and Assignments; no shared-owner login.
- Native downloadable macOS/Windows Desktop with app-managed local services and storage, no user-managed Docker; native iOS/Android paired to that same online host. Linux Desktop is future scope.
- Codex and Claude Code as initial harness support targets; qualified local-model and cloud-subscription routes, composable Git/GitHub, Email and visual-browser/computer-use abilities. Each exact route/operation remains subject to capability, containment, entitlement, egress and budget qualification.
- Scoped files and sources, bounded real PDF/editable DOCX, source-linked knowledge and immutable versioned outcomes; formal submission, trusted verification and acceptance.
- Normal human/assistant direct/group chat and separate discussion beside the exact result/report/question. A chat reply is not itself a committed work transition or permission.
- Private generated dashboards/work pages/plugins across Desktop/mobile, owned per personal/company/department scope, with test/preview/activation/edit/rollback and compatible template updates preserving local customization and data rights.
- Work home, decision attention, reports, complete usable 2D relationships and hierarchical organization chart. Color indicates real state with textual labels; graph and org chart serve different purposes. Aggregate/filter large organizations rather than render every node at once.
- Signed updates that preserve long-running work, status, governed memory, pinned prompts/checkpoints and effects; manual export, isolated restore and explicit recovery.
- English, Traditional Chinese and German UI/reports, keyboard/accessibility and mobile reading. Actual non-developer repeat-use observation is part of qualification.

These are release obligations, not evidence of current availability. Capacity for hundreds or a thousand persistent assistants is a long-term design target, not a promise of a thousand simultaneous model/browser calls.

## Autonomy and human judgment

The system handles deterministic state/permission/aggregation without model calls. Assistants decompose, coordinate, inspect and integrate within standing authority and resource ceilings. A supervisor reviews routine non-security memory/quality questions and escalates only what its mandate/context cannot resolve. Unclear scope, protected security decisions, budget increases and reserved consequential actions reach the appropriate human with one concrete target.

Increasing an assistant's permission is possible only through an explicit authorized grant, not its rank, confident output or a request to another model. Users may interact with specialists, managers or a cross-organization coordinator directly; no mandatory central chat bottleneck.

## Boundaries

Euclas is fully open-source-first, with a permissive licensing direction and useful adoption before monetization. Exact license, contribution/assets compatibility, revenue channels and publication are undecided. There is no adopted agent-count/organization-depth paywall or marketplace requirement. Do not infer profitability from user growth or promise unlimited compute/storage/support.

Optional managed Cloud hosting is separate from cloud-model use and is never automatic. Account identity, encrypted relay and updates are narrow disclosed services, not permission to host private work. The user owns routine backup decisions; there is no default managed backup schedule or hidden cloud upload.

Euclas does not expose itself as a callable ChatGPT tool/plugin. Outward use of a qualified harness or provider is a different integration. Do not extract another application's private tokens or promise unsupported reuse of every subscription.

3D graphs, voice/video calls, global cross-Instance chat federation, enterprise identity federation, arbitrary OCR/PPTX fidelity and universal provider compatibility are not silently added to the first release. Unsupported operations remain visibly unavailable.

## Success

Judge adoption by useful accepted outcomes, fidelity, repeated use, human time spent briefing/reviewing/correcting, recovery success and avoidable interruptions. Include non-business use, rejected results, dropouts and operator rescue. A demo, compilation or synthetic agent count is not that evidence.
